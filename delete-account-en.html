Delete Your Congoloz Account & Data

App: Congoloz (com.congoloz.app) • Data Controller: Tarık Buğra Pak • Contact: t.bugra.p.dev@gmail.com

1. In-App Account Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open Congoloz and go to Settings → Account Management → Delete My Account.
  2. Confirm the prompt. If your session is not recent (requires-recent-login), or if a configured server revocation build requires a fresh Apple authorization code, the app prompts you to re-authenticate with your password, Google account, or Apple account before deleting cloud records.
  3. Deletion runs in five sequential stages: (1) Your Cloud Firestore users/{uid} profile, folders/*/cards and consents subcollections, leaderboard/{uid} entry, and usernames reservation are deleted first; if cloud deletion fails, the app stops before deleting your Firebase Auth account so no orphaned cloud data is left behind. (2) Server-side Apple token revocation is checked if configured. (3) Your Firebase Authentication user record (user.delete()) is deleted. (4) Local on-device data (Hive settings, wordCards, and ai_saved_sentences_v1 boxes, your personal Gemini API key in FlutterSecureStorage, local voice recordings, custom profile photo, and iOS widget data) is wiped. (5) Provider sessions are signed out.
  4. Error Reporting: If cloud data removal, auth account deletion, local device data cleanup, or provider sign-out fails, the app shows an incomplete-deletion dialog naming the specific failed stage(s) with a Retry option (completed stages are tracked locally so you can retry from where it stopped).

2. If In-App Deletion Fails or You Cannot Access the App (Email Request)

If any in-app deletion step cannot be completed, if you want separate moderation/report records reviewed, or if you no longer have access to the app, email us from your registered email address or include your username (@username). Manual requests are processed by the data controller within a target of 7 business days (and no later than the statutory 30-day maximum).

Deletion Scope, Sign in with Apple & Technical Backups

  • Scope of Deletion: When in-app deletion completes, it removes your Firebase Auth account, users/{uid} document, folders/*/cards and consents subcollections, leaderboard/{uid} entry, usernames reservation, and—on that device—local Hive box contents (excluding the technical on-device congoloz_hive_aes_key_v1 encryption key), saved Gemini API key, local voice recordings, and custom profile photo. (For manual email requests, cloud records are deleted by the data controller; to remove on-device data, use Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear Device Data or uninstall the app.)
  • Sign in with Apple & Token Revocation: The server-side Apple token revocation endpoint (APPLE_REVOKE_ENDPOINT) is not enabled by default in unconfigured builds. In default builds, when a Sign in with Apple user deletes their account in-app, the app deletes the Cloud Firestore data, Firebase Auth account, and local device data, and then displays an “Account Deleted” dialog instructing you to remove Congoloz under iOS Settings → Apple Account → Sign in with Apple to revoke Apple ID linkage completely. (In builds where the server endpoint is configured, the app checks for fresh Apple re-authentication before deleting cloud data and stops to report the “Apple token revocation” failure if revocation errors occur.)
  • Moderation Reports & Technical Backups: Separate community safety and AI report records (reports and ai_content_reports) cannot be deleted directly by client devices; you may contact t.bugra.p.dev@gmail.com regarding those records. Records removed from active databases may also remain temporarily in Google Cloud / Firebase disaster-recovery backups and security logs until their rolling retention cycle expires.
