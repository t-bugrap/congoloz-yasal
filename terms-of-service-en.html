Congoloz Terms of Service

Last Updated: November 21, 2026 • Version: 2026-11-21 • Developer: Tarık Buğra Pak (com.congoloz.app) • Contact: t.bugra.p.dev@gmail.com

1. Age Eligibility (Global 13+)

Congoloz is designed for users aged 13 and older based on self-declared age entry (no official government ID verification is performed). Children under 13 are not permitted to create an account. If you are below the digital consent age in your jurisdiction, you must use the app with the knowledge and permission of your parent or legal guardian; the in-app guardian checkbox is a self-declaration and does not constitute identity-verified parental consent (VPC).

2. Service Scope & Virtual Economy

  • Congoloz provides flashcards, a customizable learning map with a user-selected completion duration (preset 7, 14, 30, or 60-day goals or a custom duration between 1 and 365 days), mini-games, on-device translation, pronunciation tools, and optional AI features (Google Gemini API). If the selected word count is smaller than the chosen number of days, the schedule adjusts to the available word count; the app does not guarantee language proficiency or exam success within any specific timeframe.
  • Virtual gold, trophy scores, lives, and streak freezes have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for real currency. No ads or in-app purchases are active in this version.

3. AI Usage & Content Safety

  • AI features are optional. Under Google AI Studio and Gemini API Additional Terms of Service (18+ requirement), personal Gemini API key (BYOK) entry is restricted to users aged 18 and older (18+) with explicit consent. For users aged 13–17, BYOK key entry and Google AI Studio onboarding are disabled; teen users retain full access to flashcards, the user-paced learning map (1–365 days), mini-games, on-device ML Kit translation, and safe AI Demo Mode.
  • AI outputs may contain errors; all AI screens include a “Report Response” button.

4. Community Standards & Takedown

Inappropriate usernames or rights violations can be reported directly from the Global Leaderboard or by emailing t.bugra.p.dev@gmail.com. Valid reports are reviewed within 48 hours.

5. Account Termination & Disclaimer

You may terminate your account by initiating staged account deletion in-app (Settings → Account Management → Delete My Account). In-app deletion requires an active internet connection and recent authentication (prompting re-authentication if requires-recent-login occurs); if cloud data removal, Apple token revocation, Firebase Auth account deletion, or local device data cleanup fails, the app reports the specific failed stage and allows you to retry. For Sign in with Apple users, because the server-side token revocation endpoint (APPLE_REVOKE_ENDPOINT) is not enabled by default in unconfigured builds, the app deletes your cloud and local data and notifies you to revoke Congoloz access under iOS Settings → Apple Account → Sign in with Apple. If in-app deletion cannot be completed or you cannot access the app, you may request manual deletion via the Account Deletion Page or by emailing t.bugra.p.dev@gmail.com. The app is provided “as is” without warranties of uninterrupted third-party service availability.
